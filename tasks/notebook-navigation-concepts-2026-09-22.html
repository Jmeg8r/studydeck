Make every notebook visible.

Three ways to move between the two existing study topics. Choose a concept, then select a notebook inside the mockup. These controls only change this preview.

A · Notebooks in rail. The Gemini-inspired move: a named notebook section sits above StudyDeck's existing tool navigation. Both topics stay in view while you study.

Fastest switch · compact rail

B · Split workspace. Keep tools in the original rail and give notebooks a dedicated adjacent pane. Names and status have more room, at the cost of reading width.

Strongest separation · narrower content

C · Notebook home. A destination for scanning all notebooks before entering one. It scales to many topics, but returning to the list adds a step.

Best overview · extra click
What is a notebook here? Each configured StudyDeck topic maps to an Open Notebook notebook. The names shown here come from the two current topics.
What does switching affect? Reading, Mind Map, Fact-Check, Practice Lab, Teachback, Essay and Debrief use the selected topic. Today, Flashcards and Test Center keep their cross-topic behavior.
What happens on small screens? The list becomes a horizontal notebook chooser above the page. It remains available without the desktop rail.
